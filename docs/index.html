About The MacKenzie Company

The MacKenzie Company is a public safety communications consulting firm based in Folsom, California. We specialize in helping emergency services agencies strengthen their communications operations, improve reliability, and support mission-critical decision making.

With a focus on operational readiness, compliance, and efficiency, we work closely with public safety leaders, dispatch professionals, and technical teams to deliver solutions that work in the real world — not just on paper.
